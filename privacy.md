# Privacy Policy

TikTok Ops runs locally on the owner's machine.

- OAuth tokens and browser session cookies are stored locally and are never sent
  to a third-party server.
- The app reads basic profile data and public video statistics for the accounts
  the owner explicitly authorizes.
- No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
- Data can be deleted at any time by removing the local `data/` directory.

Contact: the repository owner.
